Online play uses PeerJS (peer-to-peer). Each player keeps their own coins on their own device, so bets are on the honor system.

How to play

  • Place. Take turns placing your nine pieces on empty points.
  • Move. Once all are placed, slide a piece along a line to a neighbouring empty point.
  • Fly. With only three pieces left you may jump to any empty point.
  • Mill. Three of your pieces in a straight line let you remove one opposing piece. Pieces inside a mill are safe unless every enemy piece is in one.
  • Win. Leave your opponent with two pieces, or with no legal move.
  • Draw. Same position three times, or 60 moves without a capture.
  • Coins. You start with 500. Bets are taken when the match starts. Win to get your bet back doubled plus a bonus (AI: 10 / 25 / 50, online: 20). A draw returns the bet. 2 Players mode is just for fun.
